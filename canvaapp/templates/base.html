About Us

Canva World Tour is Coming to Nairobi! Nairobi, are you ready to create, learn and connect? The Canva World Tour 2026 is making a stop in Kenya, and we're bringing together creatives, designers, students, educators, entrepreneurs and Canva enthusiasts for a day of learning, creativity and community. Join us for an exciting in-person experience as we explore what's new in Canva, discover powerful tools and features, and learn practical ways to bring our ideas to life.
